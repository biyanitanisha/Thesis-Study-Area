  • Scaling: Band values multiplied by 0.0001
  • Usage: Detailed spatial overview and cloud-masked imagery

MODIS Vegetation Indices

  • Collection: MODIS/061/MOD13Q1
  • Resolution: 250 m spatial, 16-day temporal
  • Scaling: Raw values multiplied by 0.0001
  • Usage: Scaled NDVI dynamic spatial composite & time-series aggregation

Thesis Interpretation & Analysis

The integrated multi-sensor satellite exploration reveals significant land-cover variation and phenological trends across the study area. Sentinel-2’s high spatial resolution (10 m) provides fine-scale visual spatial distinction along urban-rural boundaries and riparian buffers, which appears aggregated or pixelated at Landsat 9's 30 m scale. However, both optical sensors remain vulnerable to seasonal cloud obscuration during the monsoon months (June–September). In contrast, the MODIS 16-day composite series offers coarse spatial resolution (250 m) but uninterrupted temporal continuity. The extracted MODIS NDVI time-series chart accurately captures distinct regional seasonal signals—peaking post-monsoon (October–November) and reaching lowest values during the late dry season (April–May). This cross-scale validation confirms MODIS's utility for continuous phenological trend tracking alongside Sentinel-2 for precise spatial boundaries in support of thesis land-monitoring objectives.
